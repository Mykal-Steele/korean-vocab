    How to use this deck

    1. Look. Each card shows the Hangul, its romanization, and the meaning in English and Thai.
    2. Listen. Press the speaker on a card, or open it and use Say the word. Tap a single syllable to hear just that block. The recordings ship with the page, so they play on any device.
    3. Write. Press Play strokes and follow the numbered order, then trace on the pad below and turn the shape off once you can write it unaided.
    4. Recall. Open Practice and pick a mode: write the meaning, write the Korean, write down what you hear in either language, or match meanings to words on a board. Then tick which sets of words to be asked about. Pick as many as you like and they get shuffled together.

    The practice modes

    한국어 → meaning
    The Korean word is shown; you write what it means. Nothing plays while you are thinking — only if you press the sound button.
    Meaning → 한국어
    The meaning is shown; you write the word in Hangul. Again, silent while the question is open.
    Listen in 한국어
    Nothing is shown at all. The Korean word plays as each question opens and you write down what you heard — press Play again as often as you need.
    Listen in English
    The same, but what plays is the meaning read out in English, and the answer is the Korean word. Both the Korean and the English are recorded and ship with the page, so neither depends on the viewer having a voice installed.
    Matching pairs
    Up to four meanings and four Korean words at a time; tap one from each column to pair them. Tapping a Korean word plays it. The set is worked through a board at a time.

    In every written mode, Check and Show answer both play the Korean word as they reveal it — right or wrong, and whichever language you were answering in. Nothing plays before that, so your ear never does the recalling for you.

    Typing Korean

    You do not need a Korean keyboard installed. In the practice drill your normal keyboard is mapped to the standard 두벌식 layout — gks types 한 — and an on-screen keyboard is there if you prefer to click. Romanization such as hanguk is accepted as well.
